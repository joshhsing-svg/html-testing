Part I：前言Part I: PreliminaryPart I: Preliminary

📄 Rule 1. Short title and commencement（简称与生效）Short title and commencementShort title and commencement

原文要点该规则名为《在线游戏推广与监管规则，2026》，自 2026年5月1日 生效。

分析生效日距离公告日（4月22日）仅 9天 过渡期，合规准备时间非常紧迫。

Key TextThese Rules are titled the "Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026" and take effect on 1 May 2026.

AnalysisThe transition period from notification (22 April) to commencement is only 9 days — an extremely tight compliance window.

📄 Rule 2. Definitions（定义）DefinitionsDefinitions

原文要点本条定义了规则使用的关键术语，包括：

  • authorised representative（授权代表）：电子社交游戏或电子竞技项目服务提供商下的授权人员；
  • Authority（管理局）：根据 Act 第8条第(1)款设立的印度在线游戏管理局；
  • Certificate of Registration（注册证书）：按第14条第(1)款颁发的注册证书；
  • determination order（认定令）：管理局依据第10条第(2)款做出的认定决定；
  • grievance（投诉）：用户就在线社交游戏或电子竞技项目的提供或可用性，就任何作为/不作为/服务/决定/做法，以书面或电子形式提出的申诉；
  • online game service provider（在线游戏服务提供商）：提供或使游戏可用的主体；
  • safeguards（保障措施）：年龄验证/年龄限制、时间限制、家长控制、用户举报与投诉处理、咨询支持、公平竞赛与诚信监控等工具。

分析

  • 首次明文将"年龄验证/年龄限制、时间限制、家长控制"纳入"Safeguards"定义范围，意味着所有在线游戏服务提供商均需具备这些机制。
  • 管理局授权范围在"Act内其他部分"涵盖 MeitY，相较草案架构更加集权（详见第四部分对比）。

Key TextThis Rule defines key terms used in the Rules, including:

  • authorised representative: a person authorised by a provider of an online social game or e-sport;
  • Authority: the Online Gaming Authority of India established under Section 8(1) of the Act;
  • Certificate of Registration: the certificate issued under Rule 14(1);
  • determination order: the order passed by the Authority under Rule 10(2);
  • grievance: a written or electronic complaint by a user regarding any act/omission/service/decision/practice concerning the offer or availability of an online social game or e-sport;
  • online game service provider: a person offering or making an online game available;
  • safeguards: tools covering age verification/age gating, time limits, parental controls, user reporting & grievance redressal, counselling support, fair-play and integrity monitoring.

Analysis

  • For the first time, age verification, time limits, and parental controls are expressly brought within "Safeguards" — implying every online game service provider must implement these mechanisms.
  • Read with other parts of the Act, the Authority's mandate consolidates power within MeitY — a more centralised architecture than the draft.

Part II：印度在线游戏管理局Part II: Online Gaming Authority of IndiaPart II: Online Gaming Authority of India

📄 Rule 3. Composition of the Authority（管理局组成）Composition of the AuthorityComposition of the Authority

原文要点管理局由 1名主席 + 6名委员 组成，全部为 职权任命（ex officio） 的政府官员：

  • 主席（ex officio）：MeitY 副部长级以上官员；
  • 委员：内政部、金融服务部、信息与广播部、青年事务与体育部、法律事务部各出1名 Joint Secretary 级官员；
  • 中央政府可任命不低于 Director 级官员担任 秘书，须有信息技术领域经验；
  • 总部设于 德里国家首都辖区，以 MeitY 附属办公室 形式运作；尽量实现 数字办公。

分析

  • 相较草案（2名Ex officio Director级Member），最终规则扩大到6名Ex officio Member，参与部门更多，监管覆盖面更广。
  • 全员由公务员组成，无独立专家或产业代表，彰显强政府监管色彩。

Key TextThe Authority consists of 1 Chairperson + 6 Members, all ex officio government officials:

  • Chairperson (ex officio): an officer of MeitY not below the rank of Additional Secretary;
  • Members: one Joint Secretary-level officer each from MHA, DFS (MoF), MIB, MYAS, and DoLA (MoL&J);
  • The Central Government may appoint an officer not below Director rank as Secretary, with IT-domain experience;
  • Headquartered in the NCT of Delhi, operating as an attached office of MeitY, with a preference for digital operations.

Analysis

  • Compared to the draft (2 ex officio Director-level Members), the final Rules expand to 6 ex officio Members, broadening inter-ministerial participation and regulatory reach.
  • An entirely civil-service composition — no independent experts or industry representatives — signalling strong state-led regulation.

📄 Rule 4. Proceedings of the Authority（议事规则）Proceedings of the AuthorityProceedings of the Authority

原文要点管理局的会议与决策规则包括：

  • 主席确定会议议程、时间和地点；
  • 会议由主席主持，主席缺席时由在场最资深委员主持；
  • 法定人数：功能编制的1/2（草案为1/3）；
  • 表决以多数票决定，票数相等时主席有决定票；
  • 成员存在利益冲突时应回避；
  • 紧急情况下主席可先行决定，并在 3天内 通知所有委员；
  • 可通过书面传阅方式决议；
  • 主席、委员或秘书可签名和印章认证管理局的命令。

分析法定人数提高至1/2、紧急通知期缩短为3天，均提升时效与代表性。

Key Text

  • The Chairperson sets the agenda, time and venue of meetings;
  • Meetings are chaired by the Chairperson, or in absence, by the senior-most Member present;
  • Quorum: one-half of the sanctioned strength (draft: 1/3);
  • Decisions by majority; the Chairperson has a casting vote in case of a tie;
  • Members with conflicts of interest must recuse;
  • In urgent cases, the Chairperson may decide first and inform all Members within 3 days;
  • Decisions may be taken by circulation in writing;
  • The Chairperson, any Member or the Secretary may authenticate orders with signature and seal.

AnalysisRaising the quorum to one-half and shortening the post-facto notice to 3 days tighten timelines and strengthen representativeness.

📄 Rule 5. Powers of the Chairperson（主席权力）Powers of the ChairpersonPowers of the Chairperson

原文要点主席享有总体监督权，可授权官员审查申请、投诉或信函，并可授权委员/委员小组执行职能。

分析赋予主席广泛的授权权力，便于日常行政运作。

Key TextThe Chairperson has overall supervisory authority, may authorise officers to examine applications, complaints or communications, and may delegate functions to individual Members or committees of Members.

AnalysisGrants broad delegation power, facilitating day-to-day administration.

📄 Rule 6. Powers and functions of the Authority（权力与职能）Powers and functions of the AuthorityPowers and functions of the Authority

原文要点除 Act 授予的权力外，管理局可：

  • (a) 维护并发布 online money games 清单；
  • (b) 维护已认定或已注册游戏的记录；
  • (c) 调查涉及在线游戏的投诉；
  • (d) 向提供/组织/协助在线游戏者，就广告、资金交易或授权发出指令；
  • (e) 与中央政府磋商后发布准则或实践守则；
  • (f) 受理对服务提供商投诉处理决定的上诉；
  • (g) 与金融机构、执法机构、政府机构协调；(h) 协助中央政府执行 Act；(i) 发布咨询建议；
  • 管理局有权修改、暂停或撤销 Act 第8条第(3)款下的指令；
  • 申请人或服务提供商提供的信息应 保密处理（除非法律或法院令要求披露）；
  • 管理局可就注册、投诉处理、用户验证、资金存款、数据保留、资金结算、公平竞赛、网络安全、用户安全、合规报告、信息提供、证书核验等发布指令/准则；
  • 管理局可要求提供额外信息。

分析

  • 赋予管理局 全链条监管权（准入—运营—资金流）。
  • 信息保密条款为商业秘密提供保护。
  • 大量具体合规细则尚未明文，由管理局后续以指令/准则发布——合规要求将 持续动态更新。

Key TextIn addition to powers under the Act, the Authority may:

  • (a) Maintain and publish the list of online money games;
  • (b) Maintain records of determined or registered games;
  • (c) Investigate grievances relating to online games;
  • (d) Issue directions on advertising, financial transactions or authorisation of funds;
  • (e) Issue guidelines/codes of practice in consultation with the Central Government;
  • (f) Hear appeals against grievance-redressal decisions of service providers;
  • (g) Coordinate with financial institutions, law-enforcement and government bodies; (h) assist the Central Government; (i) issue advisories;
  • Modify, suspend or revoke directions under Section 8(3);
  • Treat information furnished as confidential (unless disclosure is required by law or court);
  • Issue directions/codes on registration, grievance handling, user verification, fund deposit, data retention, fund routing & settlement, fair play, cybersecurity, user safety, compliance reporting, information provision, and verification of determination orders/Certificates;
  • Require additional information from service providers or any related party.

Analysis

  • Confers end-to-end regulatory power (entry — operations — money flow).
  • The confidentiality clause protects commercial secrets.
  • Many compliance details are not spelt out and will be issued via directions/codes — obligations will evolve dynamically.

📄 Rule 7. Appeals from the decision of the Authority（对管理局决定的上诉）Appeals from the decision of the AuthorityAppeals from the decision of the Authority

原文要点对下列事项不服可向 上诉机构（Appellate Authority） 上诉：(a) 第10条下的认定；(b) 第12(2)条下的注册要求；(c) 第13(3)条下 e-sport 的注册；(d) 第14条下注册证书的撤销；(e) 第20(4)条下的命令；(f) Act 第12条下的罚款。上诉期限30日；裁决期限30日；可书面、口头或主动调档审查。

分析建立了明确的行政救济路径，但上诉机构本身由 Act 规定设立。

Key TextAppeals to the Appellate Authority lie against: (a) determinations under Rule 10; (b) registration requirements under Rule 12(2); (c) e-sport registration under Rule 13(3); (d) revocation of Certificate under Rule 14; (e) orders under Rule 20(4); (f) penalties under Section 12 of the Act. 30-day filing period; 30-day disposal period; review may be written, oral or suo motu.

AnalysisEstablishes a clear administrative remedy track; the Appellate Authority itself is constituted under the Act.

Part III：在线游戏的认定Part III: Determination of Online GamesPart III: Determination of Online Games

📄 Rule 8. Determination of an online game（在线游戏的认定）Determination of an online gameDetermination of an online game

原文要点原则上服务提供商的在线游戏 无需主动申请认定，但在下列情形下须认定：(a) 管理局依职权主动发起（需先发书面通知）；(b) 服务提供商 希望将游戏作为 e-sport 提供；(c) 中央政府通过公告要求某类在线社交游戏进行认定（基于金融交易性质、规模或金额考虑）。申请方式：通过管理局网站/APP上公布的数字表格。

分析

  • 对普通在线社交游戏，原则上 无需强制认定，监管负担较轻；
  • 但中央政府可通过公告扩大认定范围，存在政策不确定性；
  • 打算作 e-sport 的游戏必须认定。

Key TextAs a general rule, a service provider's online game does not require proactive determination, except: (a) the Authority initiates determination suo motu (after written notice); (b) the service provider wishes to offer the game as an e-sport; (c) the Central Government, by notification, requires certain online social games to be determined (considering nature, scale or amount of financial transactions). Mode of application: through the digital form on the Authority's website/app.

Analysis

  • For ordinary online social games, determination is, in principle, not compulsory — easing the regulatory burden;
  • However, the Central Government can widen the scope by notification, giving rise to policy uncertainty;
  • Any game intended as an e-sport must be determined.

📄 Rule 9. Factors for determination（认定要素）Factors for determinationFactors for determination

原文要点认定游戏是否属于 online money game（在线金钱游戏）时，管理局应考虑以下要素：

  • (a) 游戏是否涉及费用、存款或其他形式的筹码/赌注；
  • (b) 用户是否对金钱或其他增益收益有预期；
  • (c) 费用的性质：(i) 是否为多人竞技/联赛的注册费、参赛费、管理费或基于表现的奖金；(ii) 是否为订阅费或一次性访问费；(iii) 是否为 赌注、押注或其他筹码（期待金钱性回报）；
  • (d) 游戏的 收入模式 结构和运营方式；
  • (e) 奖励、福利或 游戏内资产 是否可 转让、赎回、货币化或在游戏外使用。

分析

  • 对游戏内资产（如皮肤、道具、货币等）的 货币化或外部流通能力 成为关键判定因素。若某游戏内资产可在二级市场交易，可能被认定为 online money game。
  • 电子竞技联赛/赛事的注册费、奖金等 可能被视为合法赛事模式，但需设计合理。

Key TextWhen determining whether a game is an online money game, the Authority shall consider:

  • (a) Whether the game involves fees, deposits or any form of stakes/wagers;
  • (b) Whether the user expects monetary or other enhanced returns;
  • (c) The nature of the fee: (i) whether it is a registration, entry, administrative fee or performance-based prize for a tournament/league; (ii) whether it is a subscription or one-time access fee; (iii) whether it constitutes a wager, bet or other stake (in expectation of a monetary return);
  • (d) The revenue model and operating method;
  • (e) Whether rewards, benefits or in-game assets are transferable, redeemable, monetisable or usable outside the game.

Analysis

  • The monetisability or external circulation of in-game assets (e.g., skins, items, currencies) is now a key determinative factor. If in-game assets can be traded on a secondary market, the game may be classified as an online money game.
  • Registration fees, prize money, etc. for e-sport leagues/tournaments may be treated as legitimate competition models, subject to careful structuring.

📄 Rule 10. Procedure for determination（认定程序）Procedure for determinationProcedure for determination

原文要点

  • (1) 主动认定：管理局发出书面通知载明拟认定理由，征询信息并给予合理陈述机会；应申请认定：审查申请是否完整；不完整时可要求补充；补充期不计入90天审查期；审查游戏的 技术架构、玩法机制、收入模式、用户界面；必要时寻求专家或技术评估；
  • (2) 认定结果：若认定为 online money game：发布认定令并可启动 Act 下行动；若 不属于 online money game：(i) 关于 e-sport 的申请：通知申请人并按第13(3)条处理注册；(ii) 关于 social game：发布认定令；(iii) 指令服务提供商在游戏支付方式发生变更前通知管理局；
  • 认定仅针对特定游戏和特定服务提供商，其他提供商的相似游戏不自动享受同样认定；
  • (3) 认定 应在 90日内 完成（自完整申请或通知之日起算）。

分析

  • 90日审查期较为明确，但"补充期除外"使得实际时间可能延长。
  • 认定的不可传递性：相同或类似游戏，不同服务提供商仍需各自申请。
  • 支付方式变更预通知义务是重要的持续性义务。

Key Text

  • (1) Suo motu: the Authority serves a written notice stating proposed grounds, seeks information and provides an opportunity to be heard; on application: completeness review; additional-information period excluded from the 90-day clock; review covers technical architecture, gameplay, revenue model, UI; expert evaluation may be sought;
  • (2) Outcome: if online money game, issue a determination order and initiate Act action; if not: (i) for e-sport applications, notify and proceed to registration under Rule 13(3); (ii) for social games, issue a determination order; (iii) direct pre-notification of payment-method changes;
  • A determination applies only to the specific game and specific service provider; similar games by other providers are not automatically covered;
  • (3) To be completed within 90 days (from a complete application or notice).

Analysis

  • The 90-day clock is clear, but the "additional-information exclusion" may extend actual timelines.
  • Non-transferability: each provider must apply separately even for identical or similar games.
  • The pre-notification duty for payment changes is a significant ongoing obligation.

📄 Rule 11. Validity of determination order（认定令的效力）Validity of determination orderValidity of determination order

原文要点(1) 认定令在 未发生影响支付方式的变更 前长期有效；(2) 管理局可对非 online money game 的游戏定期复审；(3) 若游戏发生重大变更，管理局可：(a) 发通知暂停认定令并启动调查；(b) 调查后若认为需重新认定，可撤销原认定令并要求停止提供，直至完成新认定；或认定为 online money game 的，按第10(2)条处理。

分析认定令可因游戏变更而被 动态撤销，对发行方构成持续合规压力。

Key Text(1) A determination order remains valid for the long term, provided there is no change affecting the payment method; (2) the Authority may periodically review games determined not to be online money games; (3) on material change, the Authority may: (a) issue a notice suspending the order and initiating investigation; (b) if re-determination is warranted, revoke the original order and require cessation pending fresh determination, or treat the game as an online money game under Rule 10(2).

AnalysisA determination order may be dynamically revoked upon game changes, imposing continuous compliance pressure on publishers.

Part IV：在线游戏的注册Part IV: Registration of Online GamesPart IV: Registration of Online Games

📄 Rule 12. Requirement for registration（注册要求）Requirement for registrationRequirement for registration

原文要点原则上服务提供商的在线游戏 无需向管理局注册，但在下列情形下须注册：

  • (a) 中央政府通过公告要求 某游戏或某类游戏注册，考虑因素包括：(i) 对用户（尤其是未成年人）的伤害风险；(ii) 游戏结果性质或潜在伤害；(iii) 用户参与规模；(iv) 金融交易性质/规模/金额；(v) 服务提供商的原产国/总部所在国；(vi) 管理局认为涉及公共利益的其他因素；
  • (b) 游戏拟作为 e-sport 提供。
  • 在认定程序中若管理局认为需注册的，也需注册；online money game 不得作为 e-sport 注册；每款游戏需单独注册。

分析

  • 第 (v) 项"服务提供商的总部所在国"作为注册触发因素，对 境外游戏发行商（包括腾讯等中国企业）具有重大影响——印度政府可通过公告对特定国家的服务提供商施加强制注册义务。
  • 每款游戏单独注册意味着多个产品需逐一合规投入。

Key TextIn principle, a service provider's online game need not be registered with the Authority, except:

  • (a) The Central Government, by notification, requires a game or class of games to be registered, considering: (i) risk of harm to users (especially minors); (ii) nature of game outcomes/potential harm; (iii) scale of user engagement; (iv) nature/scale/amount of financial transactions; (v) the service provider's country of origin / country of headquarters; (vi) any other public-interest factor;
  • (b) The game is proposed to be offered as an e-sport.
  • If the Authority concludes during determination that registration is required, registration must also be obtained; an online money game cannot be registered as an e-sport; each game requires separate registration.

Analysis

  • Sub-clause (v) — "country of headquarters" as a registration trigger — is highly consequential for foreign publishers (including Chinese companies such as Tencent): the Indian government may, by notification, impose mandatory registration on service providers from specific countries.
  • Per-game registration multiplies compliance investment across product portfolios.

📄 Rule 13. Process for registration（注册流程）Process for registrationProcess for registration

原文要点(1) 通过管理局网站/APP上的数字表格申请，内容按第23条规定；(2) 管理局按第10条处理申请并做出认定；(3) 对 e-sport，若认定令签发后确认符合《National Sports Governance Act, 2025》下的识别要求，管理局应在收到完整申请后 90日内 完成注册。

分析e-sport 注册前提是 先获得《国家体育治理法》下的识别，形成双重合规门槛。

Key Text(1) Apply via the Authority's digital form (contents per Rule 23); (2) the Authority processes the application and determines under Rule 10; (3) for e-sports, once the determination order is issued and recognition under the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 is confirmed, the Authority shall complete registration within 90 days.

AnalysisE-sport registration is conditional on prior recognition under the National Sports Governance Act — a dual-compliance threshold.

📄 Rule 14. Certificate of Registration（注册证书）Certificate of RegistrationCertificate of Registration

原文要点

  • (1) 游戏完成注册后，管理局颁发带有 唯一注册号 的数字注册证书；
  • (2) 注册证书 有效期最长10年（由申请人在申请时选择）；
  • (3) 注册证书 不得在未经听证的情况下被暂停或撤销，撤销或暂停的理由包括：(a) 游戏变更可能使其被认定为 online money game；(b) 服务提供商 多次未遵守 Act 或《National Sports Governance Act, 2025》下的指令/命令/准则；(c) e-sport 情况下《国家体育治理法》下的识别失效或撤回；(d) 申请认定或注册时做出虚假或错误陈述；(e) 未支付 Act 下的罚款；(f) 违反 Act、规则或任何其他适用法律；
  • (4) 服务提供商可在任何时点主动放弃注册证书；(5) 放弃不免除此前已产生的责任或正在进行的程序。

分析

  • 注册证书有效期 由草案的5年延长至10年，降低续期频次；
  • 撤销理由较为宽泛，尤其 (f) 项"任何其他适用法律"给了管理局较大裁量空间。

Key Text

  • (1) Upon registration, the Authority issues a digital Certificate with a unique registration number;
  • (2) Valid for up to 10 years (as elected by the applicant);
  • (3) Cannot be suspended or revoked without a hearing; grounds include: (a) changes potentially making it an online money game; (b) repeated non-compliance with directions/orders/codes under the Act or the National Sports Governance Act, 2025; (c) for e-sports, lapse or withdrawal of recognition under the NSG Act; (d) false or misleading representations in the application; (e) failure to pay penalties; (f) breach of the Act, Rules, or any other applicable law;
  • (4) A provider may voluntarily surrender the Certificate at any time; (5) surrender does not extinguish accrued liabilities or ongoing proceedings.

Analysis

  • Validity extended from 5 years (draft) to 10 years, easing renewal burden;
  • Revocation grounds are broad; (f) "any other applicable law" gives the Authority wide discretion.

📄 Rule 15. Representation as determined or registered online game（合规标示）Representation as determined or registered online gameRepresentation as determined or registered online game

原文要点(a) 已认定或已注册的游戏须在 用户访问的计算机资源上显著标示 认定或注册的详情；(b) 不得：(i) 谎称某游戏为依本规则认定的"在线社交游戏"（除非已认定其非 online money game）；(ii) 谎称某游戏为依本规则注册的游戏。

分析禁止虚假认证陈述——发行方如需标榜合规身份需确保真实认证，否则构成违规。

Key Text(a) Determined or registered games must prominently display the particulars on the user-facing computer resource; (b) no person shall falsely represent a game as: (i) determined under these Rules as an "online social game" (unless so determined); or (ii) registered under these Rules.

AnalysisFalse compliance representations are prohibited — any claim of regulatory status must reflect genuine certification.

📄 Rule 16. Designation of point of contact（指定联系人）Designation of point of contactDesignation of point of contact

原文要点提供在线社交游戏或 e-sport 的每一服务提供商须依 Act 第8(3)条发布的指令/命令/准则/实践守则，指定符合条件的联系人（point of contact）并公布其联系方式。

AnalysisA compliance architecture analogous to the Grievance Officer under the IT Rules, 2021. Eligibility criteria will be specified by the Authority.

📄 Rule 17. Data retention in relation to an online game（数据保留）Data retention in relation to an online gameData retention in relation to an online game

原文要点在不减损现有法律下的数据保留要求的前提下，每一提供在线社交游戏或 e-sport 的服务提供商 须依 Act 第8(3)条下发布的指令/命令/准则/实践守则，就 流量数据、元数据或其他相关信息 在 位于印度境内的计算机资源 上进行保留和存储，保留期限和方式由该等指令具体规定。

分析

  • 🚨 重大合规要求：要求数据 在印度境内存储（data localization），对跨境游戏发行商影响巨大；
  • 具体保留期限和存储方式由管理局后续规则细化，需持续关注。

Key TextWithout prejudice to data-retention requirements under any other law, every service provider offering an online social game or e-sport shall, in accordance with directions/orders/codes/guidelines under Section 8(3), retain and store traffic data, metadata or other relevant information on computer resources located in India, with retention periods and manner to be specified in such directions.

Analysis

  • 🚨 Major requirement: data localisation in India — significantly impacts cross-border publishers;
  • Specific retention periods and storage specifications will be issued later — continuous monitoring is essential.

📄 Rule 19. Verification prior to facilitation of financial transactions or authorisation of funds（支付前核验）Verification prior to facilitation of financial transactions or authorisation of fundsVerification prior to facilitation of financial transactions or authorisation of funds

分析

  • 🚨 切断 online money game 资金流 的核心条款；
  • 对支付机构施加 即时执行义务，形成强有力的监管杠杆。

  • (2) Upon receiving an Authority direction regarding an online money game, they shall without delay: (a) suspend, restrict or stop related financial-transaction facilitation or fund authorisation; (b) provide information or assistance to the Authority.

Analysis

  • 🚨 Core provision for cutting the money flow of online money games;
  • Imposes immediate-enforcement duty on payment institutions — a powerful regulatory lever.

📄 Rule 20. Grievance redressal（投诉处理）Grievance redressalGrievance redressal

3.5 针对境外/跨境游戏发行商的特别影响3.5 Special Impact on Foreign / Cross-Border Publishers3.5 Special Impact on Foreign / Cross-Border Publishers

  • 🚨 境外发行商触发条款（Rule 12(1)(a)(v)）：中央政府在考虑注册要求时 明文将"服务提供商的原产国/总部所在国"纳入考量，赋予政府基于国别的差异化监管裁量权。
  • 🚨 数据本地化（Rule 17）：要求数据存储于印度境内，影响现有数据架构；需与 IT Rules 2021 和印度 DPDP Act 合规要求一并处理。
  • 🚨 支付链路合规（Rule 18–19）：所有支付合作方必须配合，境外发行商应评估支付渠道合规性。
  • e-sport 双重合规：若拟作为 e-sport 运营，须双轨申请（《国家体育治理法》+ 管理局注册）。
  • 🚨 Foreign-Provider Trigger (Rule 12(1)(a)(v)): The Central Government, when considering registration, expressly takes into account "the service provider's country of origin / country of headquarters", creating discretionary, country-specific regulation.
  • 🚨 Data Localisation (Rule 17): Requires in-India storage, impacting existing data architecture; must be handled alongside IT Rules 2021 and the DPDP Act.

The core policy orientation of India's PROG Rules, 2026 may be summarised as:
